## Evidence writeup by section

**1. Identity.** Title and year from the PDF header/DOI footer; first author "Waqar Aziz Sulaiman" gives `PaperID = Sulaiman_2025`.

**2. Clinical Domain.** Uses MIMIC-IV-ED (EHR triage data from Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center), predicting "inpatient mortality or ICU admission within 12 hours" from 13 triage variables. No ED-specific code exists in the controlled vocabulary, so coded `EHR` per README's guidance that EHR covers general prediction from EHR data with no more specific specialty label applicable.

**3. XAI Method/Scope.** "We used the te2rules framework to extract readable rules" (Sec. II-D) from a trained GB model — `RuleExtraction`. This is explicitly post-hoc extraction from a black-box GB ensemble ("A GB model with 100 trees was utilized as input to the te2rules framework"), not an inherently-interpretable model — flagged per taxonomy note distinguishing the two. Scope: the 43 rules "define the decision boundaries of the GB model" as a whole (model-level), so `Global`.

**4. Study Design/Participants.** No human participants anywhere in the paper — evaluation is entirely accuracy/fidelity/coverage metrics on held-out MIMIC-IV-ED data. `N_Participants = 0`, `Participant_Type = None`. `Study_Design = MethodPaper` (technical demonstration, no clinical-utility evaluation) — flagged as a judgment call against the alternative `Observational` reading.

**5. Eval_Type.** Only computational metrics reported: accuracy, AUROC, AUPRC (Table II), and te2rules fidelity plus Coverage/Consistency/Relative Coverage (Sec. III-C, Table IV) — all computed "without human judgment at any stage" → `ProxyMetric` only.
