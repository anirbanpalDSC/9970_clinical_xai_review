## Evidence writeup (summary — full rationale embedded in CSV fields above)

**Design.** Primary study is a retrospective cohort (n=184,653 ED EHR records, 2018-2022, Kaohsiung Veterans General Hospital) developing a CNN to predict ED unscheduled return visits, with a small embedded pilot survey of physicians on the SHAP-enabled interface → `Observational` (pilot treated as secondary, not primary design).

**XAI.** SHAP, both global (dataset-wide feature importance) and local (per-prediction contribution shown in the interface) → `XAI_Scope=Both`.

**Evaluation.** The only human-facing XAI evaluation is an unvalidated 2-question survey (clinical utility, trustworthiness) — genuinely borderline `Eval_Type` call, see flags. `Trust_Claim=Plausibility` (explanations described as "clinically sound"/"intuitive," no calibration-vs-model-correctness test). `Trust_Only=Yes` (survey is the sole XAI evaluation).

**Realism/EV.** `Realism_Level=2`; EV profile PV=3/TV=1/EV_E=1/OV=1 — real clinicians, artificial task/interface/outcome, matching the rubric's own worked Level-2 example.

**Method Justification.** `Mixed` (Computational primary: SHAP's theoretical/Shapley-consistency grounding is the stated reason; clinician comprehension is a downstream benefit, not the selection criterion).

**Framework Classification.** `DoshiVelez_Category=HumanGrounded` (per eval_type_taxonomy's TrustQuestionnaire→Human-grounded mapping — but see documentation-inconsistency flag). `VilonLongo_Category=HumanCentred_Qualitative` (open-ended survey, no Likert instrument).

**Reg_*.** All `No` — no comprehension test, no calibration-vs-correctness test, no user-facing uncertainty display, `Realism_Level=2`/`EV_Environment=1` below the workflow-safety threshold.
