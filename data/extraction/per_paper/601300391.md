## Evidence writeup

**Design.** Retrospective single-site cohort (SGH ED Singapore, 2009-2016, n=323,509 admissions) developing SERP, an AutoScore-derived additive point-based mortality-risk score → `Observational`, `N_Participants=0`, `Participant_Type=None`.

**XAI.** `RuleExtraction`, `Global` — SERP is inherently interpretable (the disclosed point table *is* the model, not a post-hoc approximation of a separate black box), matching the taxonomy's inherently-interpretable subtype.

**Evaluation.** `ProxyMetric` — parsimony-plot variable selection + AUC validation against 6 comparator scores (PACS, MEWS, NEWS, CART, RAPS, REMS), all computational, no human judgment of the explanation.

**Realism/EV.** `Realism_Level=1`, all EV dimensions 0 — pure retrospective computational validation, matching the rubric's own "real data alone doesn't elevate the level" miscoding warning.

**Clinician Involvement.** `Clinician_Eval=No` (no clinician evaluated the explanation); `Clinician_Design=Yes` — clinician co-authors listed under "Concept and design," and candidate variables were clinically preselected for ED usefulness.

**Quality/RoB.** All five QR dimensions = 2 — large, well-reported, STROBE-compliant, national-registry ground truth, structurally faithful explanation by construction. This is the strongest paper on data-quality grounds extracted so far, yet still has zero empirical test of interpretability itself — reinforces the review's core thesis rather than complicating it.

**Method Justification.** `Mixed`, Workflow primary — repeated deployment/ED-speed/non-specialist-usability justification, secondary comprehensibility framing.

**Framework Classification.** `DoshiVelez_Category=FunctionallyGrounded`, `VilonLongo_Category=Objective` — both mechanical, no disagreement.

**Reg_*.** All `No` — authors explicitly name prospective clinical-practice validation as future work, not yet done.
