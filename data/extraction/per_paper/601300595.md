## Evidence writeup

**XAI.** LIME, applied only as an aggregated cohort-level word-importance summary (SNR≥0.8, sign consistency≥0.6, frequency≥1% filter) → `Global` (not per-instance, despite LIME's typical local usage — coded by how it's actually used in the paper).

**Evaluation.** `ProxyMetric` — the aggregation/stability filter matches the taxonomy's explicit "explanation stability across runs (LIME instability tests)" example. No human involvement.

**Realism/EV.** `Realism_Level=1`, all EV=0 — real ED narrative text but fully computational analysis, matching the rubric's "common miscoding" real-data-doesn't-elevate-the-level note.

**Clinician Involvement.** `Clinician_Eval=No`, `Clinician_Design=No` — clinician co-design is explicitly named only as future work ("Prospective validation... co-designed with emergency physicians and nurses is the essential next step").
